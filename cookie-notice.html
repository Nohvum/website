Cookie Notice

Effective date: November 1, 2026

This Cookie Notice explains how nohvum.com (the “Site”), operated by Nohvum Pvt Ltd (“Nohvum”, “we”, “us”), uses cookies and similar technologies.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels and scripts that record how a site is used. They are commonly used to remember preferences, measure traffic and show advertising.

2. Cookies we use

The Site does not set any cookies. It also does not use analytics, advertising, social media plug-ins or other tracking technologies, and it does not store information in your browser’s local storage. Fonts, scripts and images are served from the Site itself and not from third-party networks.

Because no cookies are set, the Site does not show a cookie consent banner.

3. Third-party services

  • Hosting. The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address to deliver and secure the Site. It does not set cookies on nohvum.com for this purpose.
  • Forms. When you submit the contact or job application form, the details you enter are sent to Formspree, which forwards them to us. This happens only when you submit a form.
  • External links. If you follow a link to another site, such as LinkedIn, that site may set its own cookies under its own policy.

4. Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. Doing so does not affect how the Site works.

5. Changes to this notice

If we start using cookies or similar technologies, we will update this notice before doing so and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent. The effective date at the top shows when the notice was last revised.

6. Contact

Questions about this notice can be sent to info@nohvum.com. For how we handle personal data, see our Privacy Policy.
