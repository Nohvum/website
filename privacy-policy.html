Privacy Policy

Effective date: [effective date]

This Privacy Policy explains how Nohvum Pvt Ltd (“Nohvum”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses and protects personal data when you visit nohvum.com (the “Site”) or contact us through it. It does not cover personal data we handle on behalf of clients under a services agreement; that is governed by the agreement with the client.

1. Who we are

Nohvum Pvt Ltd is a company incorporated in India (CIN [CIN]), with its registered office at [registered office address]. For the purposes of applicable data protection law, Nohvum decides how and why the personal data described in this policy is processed.

2. Personal data we collect

We collect only what you choose to send us, plus limited technical data needed to deliver the Site.

  • Contact form. Your full name, email address, company, and whatever you write in the message fields.
  • Job application form. Your full name, email address, the role you are interested in, and the link you provide to your CV or profile, together with the information in the document or profile behind that link.
  • Emails and other correspondence. The content of messages you send us and your contact details.
  • Technical data. The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log your IP address, browser type, and the date and time of your request in order to deliver and secure the Site.

The Site does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and it does not set cookies. See our Cookie Notice.

Please do not send us sensitive personal data (for example health, financial or government identity information) through the Site.

3. How we use personal data

  • To respond to your enquiry and discuss a possible engagement.
  • To assess your application and communicate with you about roles at Nohvum.
  • To keep the Site secure and prevent spam or abuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations and to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

We do not use your personal data for automated decision-making, and we do not sell personal data.

4. Legal basis

We process personal data on the basis of your consent, given when you submit a form or write to us, and for the legitimate uses permitted by law, including complying with legal obligations. Where the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation applies, we also rely on our legitimate interests in responding to enquiries, recruiting and running our business, and on taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.

5. Who we share personal data with

We share personal data only with service providers that help us operate the Site and our business, under terms that require them to protect it:

  • Formspree, Inc. (United States) receives form submissions and forwards them to us by email.
  • GitHub, Inc. (United States) hosts the Site.
  • Our email and productivity software providers, where your messages are stored.

We may also disclose personal data to professional advisers, or to authorities where the law requires it, or in connection with a merger, acquisition or restructuring of our business.

6. International transfers

Our service providers may process personal data outside your country, including in the United States. Where the law requires it, we use appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

7. How long we keep personal data

  • Enquiries: for as long as needed to respond and to manage any resulting relationship, and no longer than [24 months] after our last contact unless an engagement follows.
  • Job applications: for the duration of the hiring process and up to [12 months] afterwards, so that we can consider you for future roles, unless you ask us to delete your details sooner.

We may keep personal data longer where the law requires it or where it is needed for legal claims.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data against loss, misuse and unauthorized access. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to give you access to your personal data, correct or update it, erase it, or stop using it, and you may withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that took place before the withdrawal. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to object to or restrict processing, to receive your data in a portable form, to nominate another person to exercise your rights, and to complain to your data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, write to [contact email]. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

10. Children

The Site is intended for business audiences and job applicants. It is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

11. Links to other sites

The Site links to third-party sites such as LinkedIn. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, not this one.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last revised. Significant changes will be made clear on the Site.
